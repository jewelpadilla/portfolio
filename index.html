A little about me

Hi! My name is Jewel Padilla, and I am a Ph.D. student in Human Factors and Applied Cognition at North Carolina State University. My research focuses on human-automation interaction , with particular interests in how people use and trust automated systems, emerging technologies, and AI-assisted decision support tools. I am especially interested in understanding how individual differences, such as expertise and perception, influence automation use and performance.

Throughout my doctoral studies, I have worked on research involving automation reliance, task-specific experiences, and the design of AI systems intended to support human decision-making in complex environments. Through these experiences, I have developed interests in experimental design, user-centered evaluation, and the development of technologies that enhance human performance while supporting effective collaboration between people and intelligent systems.
