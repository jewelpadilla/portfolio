A little about me

I'm a human factors researcher and cognitive psychology instructor. My research question, in one line: how do people decide what to look at, what to trust, and what to hand off — and how should we build technology around those answers?

I bring experimental design, behavioral measures, and a cognitive-science lens to applied problems, with a stubborn insistence that "usable" be measured, not assumed. On the side I write and review across pharmacology and toxicology, which keeps my curiosity broad and my editing sharp.
